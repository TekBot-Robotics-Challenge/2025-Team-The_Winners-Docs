Welcome to The Winners Documentation

Section titled “Welcome to The Winners Documentation”

We are The Winners, a team of 10 exceptional students from the Polytechnic School of Abomey-Calavi (EPAC) representing Benin in the prestigious Tekbot Robotics Challenge 2025 (TRC 2025) in Cotonou, Benin.

Our Mission

Section titled “Our Mission”

Our mission is to showcase EPAC’s academic excellence and innovative spirit on the international stage while contributing to the advancement of robotics and sustainable technology in West Africa. We aim to develop innovative, environmentally friendly robotic solutions that demonstrate our multidisciplinary expertise in electronics, mechanics, and computer science.

What You’ll Find Here

Section titled “What You’ll Find Here”

This documentation site serves as a comprehensive record of our journey through TRC 2025, including:

  • Team Profiles: Meet our 10-member team and their specializations
  • Project Documentation: Detailed technical documentation of our robot
  • Media Gallery: Photos and videos showcasing our development process
  • Test Results: Preselection tests and validation procedures
  • Competition Updates: Our progress and achievements
