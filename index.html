What is the Tekbot Robotics Challenge 2025?

TRC 2025 is an international university robotics competition held in Cotonou, Benin. It promotes sustainable innovation and requires teams to demonstrate multidisciplinary skills in electronics, mechanics, programming, and problem-solving.

Who are The Winners team?

We are a 10-member team from EPAC (Polytechnic School of Abomey-Calavi), consisting of 4 Electronics specialists, 3 Computer Science experts, and 3 Mechanical engineers, all working together for TRC 2025.

What are our main objectives?

Our goals include showcasing EPAC's academic excellence, promoting innovation in robotics, strengthening collaborations, enhancing practical skills, and contributing to regional development through technological solutions.

What technical challenges are we addressing?

Our robot integrates advanced electronics, sophisticated mechanical design, and intelligent programming to meet the competition's requirements for sustainable and innovative robotic solutions.

What programming technologies are we using?

We are utilizing modern embedded programming languages and frameworks optimized for robotics applications, ensuring reliable and efficient robot control systems.

How do we ensure robot reliability?

Through comprehensive testing phases including electronic circuit validation, mechanical stress testing, and software integration testing to guarantee optimal performance during competition.

What makes our approach innovative?

We focus on sustainable solutions, multidisciplinary integration, and practical applications that address real-world challenges while maintaining competitive excellence.

How can I follow our progress?

Stay updated through our documentation site, follow EPAC channels for academic updates, and visit trc.tekbot.io for official competition information.

When is TRC 2025 taking place?

The Tekbot Robotics Challenge 2025 is scheduled to take place in Cotonou, Benin. For exact dates and detailed schedule, please refer to the official competition website.

How can I support our team?

You can support us by following our documentation, sharing our progress, and encouraging STEM education initiatives in your community.

What happens after TRC 2025?

Regardless of the competition outcome, our experience will contribute to EPAC's ongoing commitment to excellence and will benefit future robotics projects at our institution.
